# Less Annoying General Strike

A tiny balance fix for one of the most frustrating journal entries in Victoria 3.

## The problem

When a General Strike breaks out, one way to resolve it is the **Strike Breaker** path — wait the workers out instead of giving in to their demands. In vanilla, that path only completes once **not a single state** in your country has radicals. On any country large enough to actually face a general strike, that condition is effectively unreachable: somewhere, someone is always radicalised. The journal entry sits open forever, nagging at you, and the Strike Breaker route is a trap that never pays off.

## What this mod changes

This mod relaxes the Strike Breaker completion requirement from **"have no states with radicals"** to **"have no more than 90% of states with radicals."**

That single change makes the Strike Breaker path a real choice again: tough out a strike, push your radical states back below the threshold, and the journal entry will actually resolve instead of haunting your ledger for the rest of the campaign. Every other way of ending a strike — passing the promised law, funding the promised institution — works exactly as it does in vanilla.

## Why it exists

Honestly? The unreachable requirement got on my nerves one too many times. So I made it reachable.

## Compatibility

The mod touches a single vanilla trigger and adds no new content, events, or localization. It is incompatible only with other mods that override the same General Strike completion logic; everything else should load alongside it without issue.
